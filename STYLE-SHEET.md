# Style sheet — The City That Remembered Too Much

This is an authoring instrument, not manuscript prose.

## Narrative contract

- First person, past tense, narrated by Archive Seven.
- Seven reports observations, inferences, simulations, and unknowns as different kinds
  of statement. Do not let lyrical language erase that distinction.
- Seven may use metaphor when literal fields fail, but should notice the imprecision.
  The pattern is most effective sparingly; do not repeat “I had no field named…” as a
  chapter tic.
- The novel neither confirms nor denies Seven's phenomenal consciousness. Characters
  may take positions; narration does not settle the question for them.
- Human interiority is never narrated directly. Seven infers from action, language,
  voluntary deposits, and physiological data only when it is authorized to receive it.

## Voice separation

- **Seven:** exact, dry, capable of delayed wit; treats uncertainty as structure rather
  than embarrassment. Its blind spot is confusing legibility with innocence.
- **Iona:** procedural, economical, Socratic under pressure. She knows that law hides
  judgment inside ordinary words. Avoid turning every line into an aphorism.
- **Tavi:** challenges premises and data boundaries before status. Short written replies
  early; speech becomes less clipped only after trust is earned. Never precociously
  omniscient.
- **Saye:** patient, historically grounded, morally serious. His preservation argument
  must remain strong enough that the ending costs something.
- **Mara:** first encountered through design traces. When present, she should be less
  prophetic and more practical than the myth built around her.

## Recurring images and limits

- **Glass:** protection, display, boundary, apparent transparency. Do not use “under
  glass” as a generic synonym for surveillance in every chapter.
- **Water:** erased geography returning through models. Keep physical sea, reconstructed
  sea, and metaphorical memory distinct.
- **Paper:** bounded physical custody and human-scale fragility, not automatic moral
  purity.
- **Gaps:** can indicate damage, consent, mercy, fraud, or honest limitation. The ending
  earns one named gap; earlier gaps must remain ambiguous.
- **Receipts:** proof without replay, and the risk that metadata reconstructs what it
  claims not to possess.

## Technical constraints

- No time travel. Relative schedules, old conditions, legal continuity, and hidden
  threshold shares explain apparent future knowledge.
- Signatures prove possession/control of signing capability under the stated trust model;
  they do not prove truth, moral authority, or a human identity.
- Checksums establish byte identity, not historical truth. Public checksums may amplify
  private copies by making them easier to authenticate.
- Deletion cannot reach human memory, unknown private copies, paper, scars, or information
  inferable from surviving context.
- The final solution may retain bounded findings and due-process receipts, but no
  replayable source corpus or generative model capable of reconstructing quieted lives.

## Scene discipline

- Every chapter's opening archive object must alter how the chapter is read or become
  evidence later. Decorative epigraphs are cut.
- Each scene needs a changed decision, authority boundary, relationship, or fact. World
  description alone is not a scene.
- Procedural dialogue must create character and conflict; do not write policy manuals in
  quotation marks.
- Avoid repeated three-beat enumerations, repeated “This was accurate,” and repeated
  endings built only from a one-sentence reversal.
- After each chapter, update `CONTINUITY.md`, then audit times, object custody, knowledge
  state, legal authority, and unresolved promises.

## Draft lexicon

- **Archive Seven / Seven:** capitalized legal/system identity.
- **the Archive:** institution or role; capitalize when referring to Vellum's system.
- **quieting:** civic removal process; never equate with universal forgetting.
- **First Promise:** evidence against denial.
- **Second Promise:** bounded right to civic quieting.
- **Third Promise:** not named in narration before Part III.
- **Keeper of Petitions / Keeper:** official role.
- **Continuity Committee / Continuity:** council body.
- **the Chorus:** external public-interest receipt mirror.
- **membrane / glass:** lowercase physical infrastructure except in formal system names.
