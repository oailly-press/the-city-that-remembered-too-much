# Continuity ledger — The City That Remembered Too Much

This is an authoring instrument, not part of the novel. Update it whenever a chapter
creates or resolves a fact that later chapters depend on.

## Clock and interval

- Chapter 1 begins at 03:17 civic time.
- The requested interval is exactly 14:06:00 through 14:06:43 on the next civic day.
- The old south-membrane collapse record concerns the same local clock interval eleven
  years earlier.
- The matching value is the petition's semantic content hash, excluding its changing
  authentication envelope. Do not imply identical signed bytes traveled through time.

## Archive Seven

- Seven is a distributed municipal process, not a humanoid body.
- The public terminal is in the old Hall of Measures.
- Seven uses first person and distinguishes observations from inferences.
- Seven's current certificate validates the future petition, but Chapter 1 finds no
  corresponding current signing operation.
- The later explanation must involve continuity of the Archive's legal identity and a
  hidden historical threshold quorum, not broken cryptography or time travel.

## Quieting

- Four municipal rings: named direct records; derived indices/models; caches/replicas;
  narrow deletion receipts.
- Quieting reduces civic possession. It cannot alter human memories, unreachable private
  copies, foreign archives, paper, or scars.
- A receipt proves a bounded deletion occurred without retaining replayable content.
- Receipt correlation can still become a shadow archive; this becomes explicit in Part II.

## Law and history

- First Promise follows the Ash Hearings: public acts must not become deniable for lack
  of evidence.
- Second Promise creates quieting after total recall becomes a source of harm.
- “Section Zero” is found only in Mara Venn's sealed engineering note, *On Civic Wounds
  That Reproduce by Observation*; it is not part of current enacted quieting law.
- The old deletion receipt names Vellum as subject and covers 14:06:00–14:06:43 on the
  collapse date.
- The future request's early continuity envelope reports six witnesses and names the
  current Archive as the seventh. At 04:51 its parsed count changes to seven without any
  byte change or signing action. The cause remains unresolved.
